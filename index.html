Research Projects

Development and Systematic Optimization of UHPLC Methods for Simultaneous Determination of Organic Acids in Plant-Based Food Matrices

2025 - Present

Focused on the development and optimization of UHPLC methods for highly polar organic acids in complex plant-based matrices. Different stationary phases and mobile phase systems, including RP and HILIC approaches, are being compared to improve retention, selectivity, and resolution. The study also includes multiple detection modes and chemometric support for method optimization.

Funded under the Scientific Research Grant Program of Eskisehir Technical University.

Development and Validation of UHPLC Method for Phytochemical Analysis in Plant Matrices

2024 - 2025

Developed and validated a UHPLC method for 7,8-Dihydroxyflavone (7,8-DHF) analysis in plant matrices after redesigning the initial workflow.
